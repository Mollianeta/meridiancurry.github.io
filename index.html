Welcome!

Welcome to Meridian Curry! Meridian Curry is your newest local destination for Japanese curry rice dishes. Whether you're a fan of mild curry or are trying to test the limits of your spice tolerance, Meridian Curry has something for everyone!

We are open Wednesday through Sunday for lunch and dinner with a variety of offerings available! Stop by for a comfortable sit-down meal or to grab some of our curry to go! Got questions about our store, curry, or something else? Check out our FAQ page for answers to some common questions. If you wanted to know more about our story, check out our About Us page as well!
