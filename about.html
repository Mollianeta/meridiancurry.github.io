About Us

Hey, I'm Severin, the owner of Meridian Curry. Japanese curry rice has been a comfort food staple through the majority of my life. The subtle sweetness, the deep savory flavors, and the warmth it brings has held a place in my heart for many years. After sharing my curry with friends and family for some time, I've come to share it with all of you too!

Meridian Curry is my attempt at that, a place where people can come to try the flavors of Japanese curry rice and fall in love with it, just as I have. I hope you stop by and give my curry a try!
